## Architecture findings

- Founder remains Product Director; engineering manages routine delivery and reports only material choices.
- Approved outcome → milestone → work item → steps is explicit, with rolling evidence-driven 1–5 candidates and no quota.
- A candidate, accepted prose record, model-edited file, report or schedule cannot mint authority. Current exact-ID grants remain in force.
- Work-item Verify, independent hosted PR/CI, and end-to-end Milestone Master Verify have distinct owners and evidence.
- Product/security/privacy sources retain their authority. Detailed Release, branch governance, installed Windows isolation and dynamic child admissibility are **future gates**, not claimed M1 achievements.
- Current runtime and root instructions remain untouched, so the old ICM is a working fallback.

## Future proof gates

M2 needs closed typed schemas, offline transition checks and migration adapters without reapproval of historical tasks. M3 needs authentic OS/connector isolation, lock/revocation/usage and GitHub governance proof. M4 needs side-by-side parity and rollback evidence. M5 requires founder-approved cutover and a separately authorized limited pilot. None is authorized by this phase.
